Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm applying for the Solutions Architect role with an unusual advantage: I've done this job at SmartRent before. From 2021 to 2024 I was a Senior Solutions Architect on this team, owning custom integrations, new-product pilots, and adoption for SmartRent's largest multifamily accounts. I know the product suite, the hardware realities, the client roster, and how Product, Engineering, Account Management, and Field Ops actually work together. I'd love to come back and put that knowledge — plus two years of new experience — to work.

The responsibilities in this posting read like my old calendar. I was the point person for third-party API and webhook integrations, managing partner–engineering communication and maintaining the API documentation. I project-managed the rollout of new smart home hardware and software across 100K+ units. I partnered with Account Managers on QBRs and expansion plans, using usage data and hardware metrics to build game plans that lifted hardware uptime to roughly 95\% and cut escalated support inquiries. And I built the customer feedback and escalation queue in Jira Service Desk that internal teams used to prioritize client needs.

What's new since I left is what I think makes me a stronger fit now. At Entrata I've spent the last year as a Business Intelligence subject-matter expert for multifamily owners and operators — living in the data side of the industry SmartRent sells into, and presenting findings from large datasets to client leadership. At Pallet I authored SOWs and risk-scoped custom implementations for enterprise logistics clients.

Most relevant to your AI expectations: I use AI tools and prompt engineering every day, not as a novelty. I built and shipped a production web application (smartdraft.app, 100+ users) almost entirely by directing Claude Code agents — writing the specs, plans, and review prompts an agent executes against, then verifying the output. I've built internal tools on the Anthropic API with tool use, published open-source Claude Code tooling, and maintain written prompting standards so AI-assisted work is repeatable and reviewable. I'd be glad to help set the team-wide AI standards this role calls for, and to model that workflow for clients who are figuring out where AI fits in their own operations.

I'm based in the Phoenix area, I still have a genuine passion for smart-home tech and rental housing, and I'd welcome the chance to talk about how I can contribute from day one.

Thank you for your consideration.
