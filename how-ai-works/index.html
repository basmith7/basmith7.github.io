    What's real here, and what isn't

    Real: the forward pass, the softmax, the cross-entropy loss, the gradients, the weight updates. Every line you see redrawn during training is an actual number that changed.

    • This network holds about 600 numbers. A model you'd actually talk to holds hundreds of billions.
    • It sees two words of context. Real models see thousands, and they weigh them against each other with attention, which this has none of.
    • It works in whole words. Real ones work in word fragments, so they can handle words they've never met.
    • On the Diamondbacks set the loss never reaches zero, and that's correct. "the diamondbacks" is followed by two different words in the text, and so is "the stadium", so the honest answer is a spread, not a single word.

    The part that carries over exactly: once training is done, the sentences are gone. Nothing stored them. What's left is a pile of numbers that happen to lean the right way, and the answer gets rebuilt from scratch every time you ask.
